    How the list works

    Ranked honestly, never for sale

    Each shop's Google rating is weighted by how many reviews stand behind it, so a perfect score from three reviews never outranks years of consistent praise. No business pays for placement, and when a detail is missing we say so instead of guessing.

    Why this directory exists

    Searching for chocolate nearby usually returns a map full of pins with no way to compare them. We gather public business data for every chocolate shop, chocolatier and candy store we can find, rank each state and city, and put the details you need in one place.
